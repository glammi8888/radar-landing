Trending Now.
GLP-1 / Ozempic Face

Search interest in GLP-1 related volume loss spiked 137% in seven days. Patients who spent months losing weight are now asking how to get their face back. Clinics with biostimulator and collagen restoration content in place are capturing every new search.

Biostimulators vs Fillers

The shift away from the overfilled look is accelerating. Sculptra, Radiesse and JULÄINE are gaining ground fast. Patients want collagen stimulation, not volume addition. Educational content explaining the difference is the highest-converting format right now.

Regenerative Aesthetics

Exosomes and PDRN from K-Beauty are landing in US clinics. PRP and PRF for hair restoration are gaining serious traction on TikTok. Patient recovery diaries and treatment explainers are the content format driving bookings in this category.
