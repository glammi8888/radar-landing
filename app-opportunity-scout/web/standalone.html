Browser build. Everything runs on this page and stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Apple is called directly via JSONP. Search volume, keyword difficulty and revenue are not shown at all. Downloads appear only as a wide range built on one assumption you control. App order is iTunes Search API relevance, not the live App Store ranking. Rating count is a proxy for competitor traction — not difficulty, downloads or demand.
One limit of this build. Apple's autocomplete endpoint needs a request header that a browser page cannot send, so demand confidence tops out at LOW here and is inferred from how many competitors target the term. The local build reaches MEDIUM. Everything else — the SERP distribution, competitor strength, traction, reviews — is identical.

Reading the table. Low competition is not opportunity. The question is “can relatively small or new apps rank here?” — which is why the Top-10 ladder is shown raw. A big incumbent at #1 does not disqualify a keyword: if 95- and 420-rating apps hold slots beside it, the door is open. Equally, a field of four tiny apps is not automatically good — it may just have no demand. Under-500 is one signal, never a requirement. Shift-click a keyword to delete it.
